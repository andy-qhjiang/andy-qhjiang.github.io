- [kirigami_sim_2D](https://github.com/andy-qhjiang/kirigami_sim_2D): An interactive simulator for rigid-deployable 2D kirigami patterns.
- [mexIPOPT](https://github.com/andy-qhjiang/mexIPOPT): A MATLAB interface for IPOPT.
