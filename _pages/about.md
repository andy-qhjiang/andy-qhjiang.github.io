I am a PhD student in the [Department of Mathematics](https://www.math.cuhk.edu.hk/) at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by [Prof. Gary P. T. Choi](https://garyptchoi.github.io/group.html).

My research focuses on the design of shape-morphing metamaterials, with particular interest in kirigami structures and their mathematical and computational modelling.
